# French restaurant SaaS landscape (2024-2026) — competitors, gaps, and white spaces vs US-proven models

Context: TK Digital (Tarik, Nord de France) currently sells to halal restaurants / pizzerias / kebabs / snacks a "Bascule Directe 90 jours" offer: optimized Google Business Profile, direct-ordering website (click & collect / WhatsApp ordering), QR codes on tables, loyalty card (10th dish free), WhatsApp marketing campaigns, automated review collection, 6-month minimum commitment (source: /home/user/tk-digital/restaurateurs.html). This is essentially a hand-built, service-heavy "Owner.com-lite" for fast-food independents.

Research method note: ~22 search/fetch calls. Two fetches were blocked by the egress proxy (resatable.fr, nerolia-ai.fr), so some figures rely on search-result snippets of third-party comparison/blog sites rather than primary vendor pricing pages. Such figures are flagged "(secondary)".

## Q1. Main French players per category: pricing, funding, number of clients

### Takeaway
Every category in the brief has at least 2-5 funded French/European players, but they cluster on chains, multi-site groups and upmarket table-service restaurants (Inpulse, Malou, Zenchef, Sunday, Skello, Lightspeed). Pricing for "serious" tools is typically €80-€350/month per tool; a low-cost long tail (€20-€90/month) exists mostly from small, unfunded French startups.

## Q2. Adoption of digital tools among French independent restaurants

### Takeaway
Hard, restaurant-specific adoption rates per tool category are scarce. Available data: 88% of restaurant units are independent; ~78% of restaurateurs used digital tools daily (2022) but 80% feel their transition is unfinished; only 20% of hospitality TPE/PME use AI (2025).

### Cited Findings
- Gira: 415,595 food-service units in France in 2025 (+2.1% YoY), a very broad definition (incl. collective catering, bakeries, vending); independents ~88%, chains ~12% — [Extencia / Gira summary](https://www.extencia.fr/chiffres-cles-restauration); [Modèles de business plan](https://modelesdebusinessplan.com/blogs/infos/nombre-restaurants-france) (secondary). The ~175,000 figure in the brief corresponds to the narrower "restaurants" (INSEE-style) count; not re-verified here.
- Chains expanded their networks in 2024 — [Franchise Magazine](https://www.franchise-magazine.com/news-franchise/enquete-chaines-restauration-rapide-juin-2025)
- 2022 GNI survey: 78% of restaurateurs use digital tools daily; 80% believe their digital transition is not finished — [Reservit](https://www.reservit.com/digitalisation-restaurants/) (secondary citation of GNI)
- France Num 2025 barometer (hébergement-restauration TPE/PME): 20% use AI solutions (+12 pts YoY) vs 26% all sectors; 72% believe online presence directly increases customer base — [France Num](https://www.francenum.gouv.fr/guides-et-conseils/strategie-numerique/comprendre-le-numerique/barometre-france-num-2025-le-0)
- Scale proxies: Zenchef 25,000 clients (20 countries), Skello 25,000 (all sectors), Zelty 4,000+, Malou 3,500+, Sunday 1,500 in France, Inpulse 1,500+ — sources in Q1.

### Inferences
- Relative to ~175k restaurants, even the biggest vertical players (Zenchef, POS vendors) reach low double-digit % penetration; review management (Malou 3,500) and food-cost tools (<~2,000 each) are in low single-digit % of independents — large headroom. (Inference from client counts, which include non-French and chain sites.)
- POS is near-universal due to legal cash-register obligations, but "advanced" layers (food cost, CRM, AI phone) are early.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2024-2026 per-category adoption survey (e.g. % with own ordering website, % using review software) for French independents was found.

## Q3. Where are the gaps (US model not yet replicated in France)?

### Takeaway
The clearest white space is a French "Owner.com": an all-in-one, done-for-you SEO website + commission-free ordering + automated marketing (SMS/WhatsApp/email) + loyalty + Google profile for independents, sold on a flat subscription. Pieces exist separately (MyResto, ClickEat, Obypay, Malou, Zenchef) but nobody bundles them with a growth-marketing promise for fast-food independents. A French MarginEdge and French AI phone agents are emerging but fragmented and pre-scale.

### Cited Findings
- No French Owner.com equivalent surfaced in search; alternatives listed are US (Toast, ChowNow) — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/owner-com/competitors/alternatives)
- Commission-free ordering tools are cheap commodities (€20-€50/month) but mostly software-only, not marketing-led — [OrdraFood](https://www.ordrafood.fr/commande-en-ligne-restaurant); [Collectly](https://collectly.fr/blog/commande-en-ligne-restaurant-sans-commission); [Commande-Restaurant](https://commande-restaurant.com/); [MyResto](https://www.my-resto.net/solutions-digitales/reservation-en-ligne/)
- Marketing/reputation leader Malou charges €169/month self-serve and €549-599/month done-for-you, oriented to trendy/Parisian and chain restaurants — [Revyo](https://www.getrevyo.com/blog/alternative-malou); [Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2023/11/20/foodtech-malou-leve-10-millions-deuros-pour-simposer-a-paris-et-a-new-york/)
- "MarginEdge-like" (invoice → food cost) for independents: only small 2025-2026 entrants (RestoMaestro €39, Onrush €89, Recepta, Cocotte); funded leader Inpulse targets chains — [RestoMaestro](https://restomaestro.com/blog/meilleurs-logiciels-food-cost/); [Onrush](https://onrush.fr/food-cost/meilleur-logiciel-food-cost-restaurant); [Inpulse/Néo-restauration](https://www.neorestauration.com/article/inpulse-leve-7-millions-pour-ameliorer-la-marge-des-chaines-de-restauration-grace-a-l-ia,68399)
- AI phone agents in French: Zenchef Call AI (€99/month, 500 min, reservations only, requires Zenchef) plus ~8 small startups (Sylen €49/month, Yumcall, Natalia, AirAgent, Vocalis…) — sources in Q1. None of those found handle phone *takeaway orders* pushed into a French POS as a documented core feature (Vocalis mentions "réservations et commandes" — [Vocalis](https://vocalis.pro/agent-vocal-ia-restaurant)).

### Inferences
- White space #1 (best fit for TK Digital): "Owner.com for French fast-food independents" — site + direct ordering (web/WhatsApp) + Google profile + review automation + loyalty + automated SMS/WhatsApp campaigns, flat €99-199/month, done-for-you. TK Digital already delivers this manually; the gap is productization/automation. Could be assembled by reselling/white-labeling a €20-50 ordering engine (OrdraFood/Collectly/ClickEat-type) and adding the marketing layer.
- White space #2: AI phone agent for takeaway/delivery phone orders (pizzerias/kebabs take many phone orders) in French, feeding the ordering system — Zenchef's product is reservation-centric and table-service oriented; the fast-food phone-order use case looks under-served. Reselling one of the small French voice startups (€49-99/month) is a quick option.
- White space #3: French MarginEdge — cheap invoice-scan-to-food-cost for independents, especially bundled with the September 2026 e-invoicing reception obligation (structured supplier e-invoices make automated food cost much easier). Market is nascent; players are tiny.
- Reservations (Zenchef, TheFork), HR scheduling (Skello, Combo) and POS are crowded/well-funded — poor targets for a small agency except as referral/affiliate partners.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether Obypay, Zelty or Innovorder offer automated marketing campaigns comparable to Owner.com's.
- No French data on share of orders taken by phone in fast-food independents.
- US-side pricing (Owner.com ~$499/month, MarginEdge ~$330/month) not verified in this pass — covered by other researchers presumably.

## Q4. Regulatory constraints creating opportunities

### Takeaway
Two regulatory triggers matter in 2026-2027: (1) mandatory e-invoice *reception* for all VAT-registered businesses from 1 Sept 2026 and *emission* for SMEs/TPE from 1 Sept 2027; (2) the cash-register anti-fraud rule, where the 2026 finance law (21 Feb 2026) restored editor self-attestation instead of mandatory third-party NF525/LNE certification, with a €7,500 fine per non-compliant system.

### Cited Findings
- Every restaurant subject to VAT must be able to receive electronic invoices from 1 Sept 2026, regardless of size; emission required for large/mid-size companies from 1 Sept 2026 and for SMEs, TPE, micro-entrepreneurs from 1 Sept 2027 — [Pennylane](https://www.pennylane.com/fr/fiches-pratiques/facture-electronique/facturation-electronique-pour-les-restaurants-ce-quil-faut-savoir); [economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/tout-savoir-sur-la-facturation-electronique-pour-les-entreprises); [UMIH FAQ](https://www.umih.fr/medias/news/foire-aux-questions-la-facturation-electronique.html); [Shine](https://www.shine.fr/blog/facturation-electronique-restaurant/)
- Treatment of restaurant bills (notes de restaurant) changes from 1 Sept 2026 — [LégiFiscal](https://www.legifiscal.fr/actualites-fiscales/4625-notes-restaurant-comment-traiter-1er-septembre-2026.html)
- POS vendors (Lightspeed, L'Addition, Innovorder, Tiller) are aligning with Plateformes Agréées — [Infos PA](https://www.infos-pa.com/articles/logiciels-caisse-restauration-lightspeed-laddition-innovorder-tiller-pa)
- Cash registers: 2025 finance law had removed editor self-attestation (16 Feb 2025) requiring third-party NF525/LNE certification; the 2026 finance law re-established the editor attestation (21 Feb 2026), so third-party NF525 is not mandatory; many 2025 articles are now outdated; fine €7,500 per non-compliant system — [Agiris](https://www.agiris.fr/articles/logiciel-de-caisse-certifie-nf525-lne-obligations); [Shopcaisse](https://www.shopcaisse.com/blog/attestation-logiciel-caisse-retablie-2026); [Tactill](https://www.tactill.com/blog/loi-norme-des-caisses-2026-ce-qui-change-vraiment/); [economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/entreprises/gerer-son-entreprise-au-quotidien/gerer-sa-comptabilite-et-ses-demarches/ce-quil-faut-savoir-sur-la-certification-des-logiciels-de-caisse)
- RGPD: French voice-AI vendors explicitly market RGPD compliance (e.g. Vocalis) — [Vocalis](https://vocalis.pro/agent-vocal-ia-restaurant). Restaurants keeping customer data ownership is a selling point vs platforms (Zenchef) — [Resatable](https://resatable.fr/blog/combien-coute-zenchef-restaurant) (secondary).

### Inferences
- E-invoicing reception (Sept 2026) means supplier invoices will increasingly arrive as structured data — lowering the cost of building/reselling an automated invoice-to-food-cost tool for independents, and creating an "accompagnement conformité" selling moment until the Sept 2027 emission deadline.
- The NF525 relaxation reduces the regulatory barrier for new/cheap POS entrants, but the €7,500 fine still pushes independents with old/uncertified tills to switch — an upsell/referral occasion (e.g. SumUp/Tiller affiliate).
- RGPD + consent rules apply to SMS/WhatsApp marketing: any automated-marketing product must collect opt-in (relevant to TK Digital's WhatsApp campaigns). Not researched in detail — CNIL guidance should be checked.

### Gaps
- No CNIL-specific guidance for restaurant SMS/WhatsApp marketing or call recording by AI agents was fetched.
- Whether AI phone agents must disclose they are AI under the EU AI Act (transparency obligations from Aug 2026) was not verified.
