## 1. Owner.com: ARR, valuation, growth, value proposition, sales model

### Takeaway
Owner.com is the reference case: about $34M ARR (2024), then $81M (Series C, May 2025, $1B valuation), then $100M ARR (July 2026) and a $240M Series D at a $2.3B valuation (Aug 2026). It sells a flat-fee, all-in-one "commission-free" direct-ordering, website, app and marketing stack to independents. It has since rebuilt around AI "executives" (AI CMO/CFO/CTO) and AI phone ordering. The founder says the core retention risk is proving ROI within 30 days.

### Cited Findings
- Series C: $120M co-led by Meritech and Headline at a $1B valuation (May 2025) — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/tech-supplier-ownercom-raises-120m-giving-it-1b-valuation); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/restaurant-tech-owner-com-raises-094941935.html)
- ARR of $34M in 2024 and $81M at the time of the Series C (about 12.3x ARR multiple). This is a third-party estimate — [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/owner.com). A Substack analysis frames a round as "23x ARR vs closest public comp at 2.6x" — [Capitaly](https://capitaly.substack.com/p/owner-raised-at-23x-arr-the-closest)
- Series D: $240M led by Goldman Sachs Alternatives (Growth Equity) at a $2.3B post-money valuation, with Meritech, Redpoint, Headline and Jack Altman. Owner crossed $100M ARR in July 2026 — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/147543-owner-raises-240m-series-d-at-2-3b-valuation-to-bring-ai-to-local-busine/); [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/owner-raises-240m-ai-native-restaurant-tech); [Adam Guild on X](https://x.com/adamguild/status/2093375217120784579); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/owner-raises-240m-led-by-goldman-sachs-alternatives-to-build-the-ai-native-platform-for-every-local-business-302862420.html)
- Owner expects restaurants to process more than $1B in sales through the platform in 2026 — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/owner-com-did-an-ai-rebuild-to-accelerate-past-100m-arr-6-lessons-and-what-it-costs-to-apply-them/)
- Use of funds: serve every independent US restaurant, expand internationally, and extend to salons, spas and independent grocers. The product scope is websites, online ordering, mobile apps, CRM, customer support, POS and AI phone ordering — [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/09/03/owner-secures-240m-series-d-at-2-3b-valuation-to-expand-ai-platform-for-local-businesses/)
- Pricing: flat $499/mo per restaurant for the full feature set, with no contracts or tiers. There is also a $249/mo plan plus a 5% restaurant fee per order — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/owner). A 2026 third-party write-up names these "Flexible" ($249 + 5%) and "Flat Rate" ($499, recommended at $5k+/mo in online sales) — [growwithba](https://growwithba.com/blog/owner-vs-doordash)
- Delivery: 0% commission on direct orders. Delivery goes through DoorDash/Uber drivers at about a $7 flat fee per delivery, passed through at cost — [growwithba](https://growwithba.com/blog/owner-vs-doordash); [Owner delivery page](https://www.owner.com/delivery)
- Positioning vs DoorDash: marketplaces take about 15-30% per order and own the customer. Owner offers a branded site, app and SMS/email to turn marketplace diners into direct regulars — [growwithba](https://growwithba.com/blog/owner-vs-doordash); [Sauce on Owner pricing](https://www.getsauce.com/post/owner-com-pricing-fees)
- AI pivot: more than 83% of new customers now start their journey inside an AI product. The "AI Executives" launch (AI CMO, CFO, CTO a restaurant can "hire") reframed Owner from marketing automation to an "operating system" for independents — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/owner-com-did-an-ai-rebuild-to-accelerate-past-100m-arr-6-lessons-and-what-it-costs-to-apply-them/); [cloud.substack](https://cloud.substack.com/p/ownercom-did-an-ai-rebuild-to-accelerate)
- Unit-economics risk as seen by the CEO: single-location and small-chain customers have smaller LTVs and higher churn. Quote: "For local SMBs, if they don't see an ROI in the first thirty days, they cancel." — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/owner) (snippet); see also [Antoine Buteau, Lessons from Adam Guild](https://www.antoinebuteau.com/lessons-from-adam-guild/)
- Founder story (scrappy tactics, post-COVID pivot to product-market fit) — [First Round podcast](https://review.firstround.com/podcast/scrappy-tactics-and-a-huge-post-covid-pivot-owners-unconventional-journey-to-product-market-fit-adam-guild-co-founder-and-ceo-of-owner/); [Forbes 2025](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stephenpastis/2025/05/13/this-tech-startup-is-using-ai-to-help-local-restaurants/)

### Inferences
- The math of the "commission-free" pitch explains the $5k/mo threshold. At a 25% marketplace commission, shifting $2k/mo of orders to direct saves about $500/mo, which covers the $499 fee. At $5k/mo online sales, the flat plan beats the 5% plan ($249 + $250).
- The ARR multiple fell from about 12x (Series C) to about 23x (Series D, $2.3B / $100M). Investors pay for AI-led growth and the multi-vertical expansion story.
- The "ROI within 30 days" rule implies onboarding must produce a visible early win: a live site, first direct orders, or a Google ranking lift.

### Gaps
- Could not read the full Owner Series C memo (owner.com/c) or Sacra's page, because fetch was blocked. I found no confirmed figures for customer count, logo churn, CAC, payback, or sales-team structure (reported to be heavily outbound/SDR plus demo, but not verified here).
- No verified data on SEO/Google-ranking uplift claims.

## 2. Toast: field sales, fintech take rate, payback

### Takeaway
Toast pairs a large local field-sales and referral machine with payments monetization. In FY2025 it had more than $2.0B ARR (Subscription $1.06B, Payments $986M), about 164k locations, a net take rate of about 58 bps, 109% SaaS net revenue retention, and a CAC payback in the "mid-teen months."

### Cited Findings
- ARR above $2.0B (+26%) at 12/31/2025: Subscription ARR $1,061M (+28%), Payments ARR $986M (+24%). About 164,000 live locations (+22%), with a record 30,000 net adds in 2025 (about 8,000 in Q4) — [BusinessWire, Toast FY2025 results](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260212058106/en/Toast-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results)
- Q4 2025 fintech gross profit +25% YoY, net take rate 58 bps, payments take rate 48 bps (+2 bps). SaaS gross margin 80% in Q4. SaaS net revenue retention 109% for 2025 — [Yahoo Finance Q4 2025 call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/toast-inc-tost-q4-2025-050116070.html); [Cust.co](https://cust.co/companies/toast/)
- Dollar-based CAC payback stayed in the "mid-teen months" in 2025. Core payback fell from 22 months (2019) to 14 months (2023). New markets are above core but on a path to under 20 months. Levers were higher ARPU from more modules, referrals, and rep productivity — [Motley Fool Q4 2025 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/12/toast-tost-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/)
- A third-party quant site claims Toast's incremental CAC payback was "negative" in Q2 2026 — [StockStory](https://stockstory.org/us/stocks/nyse/tost/news/earnings/toast-nysetost-surprises-with-q2-cy2026-sales-2). This is a proprietary metric that conflicts with management's framing; treat with caution.
- Early investor view of Toast's model (payments-attached POS) — [Bessemer memo](https://www.bvp.com/memos/toast)
- Toast financing (Toast Capital) and hardware-as-a-service are described as improving LTV and reducing churn. This is a secondary source — [PortersFiveForce](https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/competitors/toasttab)

### Inferences
- The subscription and payments split (about 50/50 ARR) shows the fintech attach model at work. Payments turns a roughly $100-300/mo SaaS customer into a much larger revenue stream, but it requires owning the POS/payments rail. A small agency cannot replicate this.
- Mid-teen-month payback with field sales is only viable because of payments revenue and 109% NRR. A pure-SaaS SMB tool at $300-500/mo needs a far cheaper acquisition channel.

### Gaps
- Toast does not disclose logo churn. I found no reliable public figure.

## 3. Point solutions: 7shifts, MarginEdge, Popmenu

### Takeaway
Point solutions sell a hard-dollar ROI tied to one cost line: labor (7shifts, "save $500/mo"), food cost and back office (MarginEdge, $350/location/mo), or marketing and phones (Popmenu). All of them integrate heavily with each other and with the POS.

### Cited Findings
- 7shifts ROI claim: save "$500/month on labor." Its ROI calculator and labor-cost calculator are used as lead magnets — [7shifts ROI calculator](https://www.7shifts.com/calculators/roi-calculator/); [7shifts labor calculator](https://www.7shifts.com/restaurant-labor-cost-savings-calculator)
- MarginEdge pricing: $350/location/mo, with a 10% discount on annual billing; the Freepour add-on is +$150/location/mo — [MarginEdge pricing](https://www.marginedge.com/pricing/)
- MarginEdge value proposition: real-time food and labor cost, automated invoice processing, inventory and recipes, and daily P&L. It integrates with 50+ tools including 7shifts (labor data flows into the P&L) — [MarginEdge food cost](https://www.marginedge.com/food-cost); [Hospitality Tech](https://hospitalitytech.com/marginedge-7shifts-announce-integration)
- MarginEdge raised a $45M Series C (Dec 2022, Ten Coves Capital), for more than $70M total — [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/MarginEdge-raises-45M/638308/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/07/marginedge-45m-restaurant-spend/)
- Popmenu: plans from about $149/mo; AI Answering reported at about $528/mo combined. It takes orders and reservations by sending an SMS link rather than closing the order on the call — [Certus review](https://www.certus-ai.com/blogs/popmenu-review); [Loman vs Popmenu (competitor)](https://loman.ai/blog/loman-vs-popmenu-restaurant-voice-ai); [Popmenu AI answering](https://get.popmenu.com/solutions/ai-phone-answering)
- Popmenu case data: Next Level Brands had 58,457 calls answered, 44% revenue-related, saving 1,184 staff hours. Max's Restaurant had more than 329,000 calls handled and more than $1.5M in online orders — [Certus review, citing Popmenu](https://www.certus-ai.com/blogs/popmenu-review)

### Inferences
- The ROI message maps to a price anchor: tools priced at $150-500/mo claim savings of at least 1-3x the fee on a line the owner already watches (labor %, food cost %, commissions).

### Gaps
- I found no verified MarginEdge "X% food cost reduction" figure, and no 7shifts ARR or customer count for 2025.

## 4. AI phone agents: missed calls, revenue recovered, pricing

### Takeaway
The dominant pitch is "you miss 30-60% of calls at peak, and about $20-29k/year per location walks away." Independent-focused products cost about $199-599/mo per location. Enterprise QSR vendors (ConverseNow, Kea) sell on upsell and labor-hour metrics instead. Almost all statistics come from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- Slang.ai's dataset, drawn from millions of calls: 60% of calls go unanswered during peak hours, 71% of restaurant calls are revenue-related, and 85% of callers who hit voicemail do not call back — [Slang AI](https://www.slang.ai/post/unanswered-restaurant-phone-calls-revenue)
- About 150 missed calls/month on average, with 43% during the 7-9 PM rush. The industry loses an estimated $20B/year, about $28,700 per restaurant, based on TouchBistro research and NRA location counts — [QSR Pro](https://qsr.pro/articles/restaurant-ai-phone-ordering-missed-calls-20-billion-revenue-2026); [Hostie](https://hostie.ai/resources/missed-calls-cost-restaurants-43-percent-revenue-ai-phone-answering-2025)
- About 30% of calls go unanswered at peak — [Loman blog](https://loman.ai/blog/restaurant-ai-phone-answering)
- Hostie (vendor) claims restaurants miss 58% of lunch-rush calls, that voice AI cuts missed calls by 80%, and that it adds $27K per location. It also cites a 500k-call study showing missed calls falling from 36% to 3% — [Hostie](https://hostie.ai/resources/restaurant-missed-calls-voice-ai-solution-2025-data); [Hostie 500k study](https://hostie.ai/resources/hostie-ai-500k-call-study-missed-calls-reduction)
- Pricing:
  - Slang.ai: Core $399/mo, Premium $599/mo per location, aimed at reservations and FAQs — [DirectOrders](https://www.directorders.com/blog/ai-tools-for-restaurants); [ailearningguides](https://ailearningguides.com/ai-restaurant-phone-ordering-agents-2026/)
  - Loman: "starting at $199/mo," live in under 24 hours; a later comparison lists about $349-599/mo — [Loman](https://loman.ai/); [DirectOrders](https://www.directorders.com/blog/ai-tools-for-restaurants)
  - DirectOrders Pro + Voice: $349 with 500 AI minutes — [DirectOrders](https://www.directorders.com/blog/ai-tools-for-restaurants)
- ConverseNow (QSR, phone and drive-thru): claims up to 31% same-store sales lift, 25% higher tickets, and about 90 labor hours recovered per store per month, across more than 1,200 restaurants in 40 states. Its partners include Olo and Deliverect — [Bland AI roundup](https://www.bland.ai/blog/ai-voice-agents-for-restaurants); [ConverseNow](https://conversenow.ai/); [QSR Web](https://www.qsrweb.com/news/conversenow-deliverect-partner-to-integrate-voice-ai-into-restaurant-management/)
- Revmo handles voice, text and email for reservations and waitlists in 76 languages, aimed at multi-unit and franchise operators. Kea targets 5-50 location QSR brands with 1,500+ phone orders per month per location — [Bland AI roundup](https://www.bland.ai/blog/ai-voice-agents-for-restaurants); [Kea blog](https://kea.ai/blog/the-top-9-ai-phone-ordering-systems-to-evaluate-in-2026); [Revmo blog](https://revmo.ai/blog/best-ai-voice-tools-for-restaurants-2025)
- AI agents can handle about 80% of routine calls without a human (vendor claim) — [Bland AI](https://www.bland.ai/blog/ai-voice-agents-for-restaurants)

### Inferences
- Using the ROI-to-price ratio as a selling device: a $28.7k/yr lost-revenue claim against a $4.8-7.2k/yr fee gives the "4-6x ROI" story. Recovered orders are directly attributable (the call leads to a POS order), which makes this one of the easiest ROIs to prove within 30 days.
- Segmentation: independents get Loman/Slang/Popmenu at $199-599 flat. Chains get ConverseNow/Kea/Revmo on per-location enterprise contracts.

### Gaps
- I found no independent (non-vendor) audit of missed-call rates. The $20B figure traces back to vendor use of TouchBistro and NRA data. Enterprise pricing for ConverseNow, Kea and Revmo is not public.

## 5. Churn, retention, CAC, and payback benchmarks for SMB restaurant SaaS

### Takeaway
There are no public logo-churn figures for restaurant SaaS. Generic SMB SaaS benchmarks run 3-7% monthly churn (roughly 31-58% annually). Toast shows that module expansion plus payments can push NRR to 109% and payback into the mid-teen months. Retention hinges on ROI proof within 30 days and on becoming system-of-record or embedded (POS, payments, data integrations).

### Cited Findings
- SMB SaaS: 3-7% monthly churn is acceptable only if expansion outpaces logo loss, and small-business segments show 31-58% annual churn — [Genesys Growth](https://genesysgrowth.com/blog/saas-churn-rates-stats-for-marketing-leaders); [Growigami](https://growigami.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks)
- Average B2B SaaS churn is about 3.5% monthly (2.6% voluntary, 0.8% involuntary) — [Genesys Growth](https://genesysgrowth.com/blog/saas-churn-rates-stats-for-marketing-leaders). This is a secondary aggregator of unclear methodology.
- Median public SaaS NRR is 110-115%; Toast's is 109% — [SubJolt](https://www.subjolt.com/guides/nrr-grr-benchmarks/); [Cust.co](https://cust.co/companies/toast/)
- Owner's CEO says SMBs that see no ROI in 30 days cancel, and that single-location customers carry higher churn and lower LTV — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/owner)
- Toast: payback in the mid-teen months; ARPU expansion through modules and referrals — [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/12/toast-tost-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Embedded fintech raises revenue per customer by 2-5x, with high-frequency verticals like restaurants at the upper end, and improves churn and LTV/CAC — [a16z, Fintech Scales Vertical SaaS](https://a16z.com/fintech-scales-vertical-saas/); [Gusto Embedded](https://embedded.gusto.com/blog/embedded-fintech-strategy-revenue-churn/)

### Inferences
Features that plausibly reduce churn, based on the cited mechanisms:
- Attributable revenue dashboards, such as "orders recovered by AI" or "commissions saved."
- Owning the customer database: CRM, SMS lists and loyalty programs create switching costs.
- Integrations into the POS and the P&L.
- Bundling several modules, which raises NRR.
- Payments or financing attach.

Restaurant closures (a high base rate among independents) put a floor under logo churn that no product can fix.

### Gaps
- I found no public logo-churn figure for Toast, Owner, Popmenu, 7shifts or MarginEdge. I found no CAC figures for Owner or the AI-voice startups.

## 6. Which ROI messages convert best; bundling vs point solutions; the role of AI in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
The winning messages are concrete dollar recovery or savings tied to a pain the owner already feels: "Stop paying DoorDash 30%" (Owner), "You're missing X calls = $Y/yr" (voice AI), "Save $500/mo labor" (7shifts), "real-time food cost" (MarginEdge). The market is moving toward AI-native bundles, such as Owner's "AI executives" and Popmenu adding AI answering, with point AI agents as a wedge.

### Cited Findings
- Owner's commission-savings framing (0% vs 15-30%) and flat-fee simplicity (no contracts, no tiers) — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/owner); [growwithba](https://growwithba.com/blog/owner-vs-doordash)
- Missed-call revenue framing ($28.7k per location per year, 150 missed calls/mo) is the headline across AI-voice vendors — [Slang AI](https://www.slang.ai/post/unanswered-restaurant-phone-calls-revenue); [QSR Pro](https://qsr.pro/articles/restaurant-ai-phone-ordering-missed-calls-20-billion-revenue-2026)
- Bundling trend:
  - Owner expanded to POS, CRM, support and AI phone ordering, with 83% of new customers starting in an AI product — [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/09/03/owner-secures-240m-series-d-at-2-3b-valuation-to-expand-ai-platform-for-local-businesses/); [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/owner-com-did-an-ai-rebuild-to-accelerate-past-100m-arr-6-lessons-and-what-it-costs-to-apply-them/)
  - Toast's model is module expansion — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/12/toast-tost-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Vertical AI: Bessemer argues vertical AI could reach 10x the market cap of legacy SaaS. Early vertical-AI companies reportedly grow about 400% YoY at 65%+ gross margin, though this is a secondary summary — [Bessemer, future of AI is vertical](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/part-i-the-future-of-ai-is-vertical); [BVP State of AI 2025](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-state-of-ai-2025)

### Inferences
- Rough ranking by ease of proof for independents: (1) missed-call recovery, which is directly counted; (2) commission savings, which are calculable from the DoorDash statement; (3) labor savings; (4) food-cost % reduction, which is real but takes months to show and needs discipline.
- Point solutions win the first sale; bundles win retention and NRR. A small player should use one wedge that proves ROI in 30 days, then cross-sell.

### Gaps
- I found no A/B or conversion-rate data comparing ROI messages; the ranking above is inference.

## 7. Feasibility for a small agency/startup (no-code, LLM APIs, Vapi/Retell/ElevenLabs)

### Takeaway
AI phone answering and ordering is the most replicable product. Raw voice costs are about $0.13-0.31/min against $199-599/mo retail prices, so gross margins are high if call volume is moderate. POS integration and reliable order capture are the hard parts. Websites, SEO, online ordering and SMS/email marketing (a lighter Owner) can be assembled or white-labeled. Payments/POS (Toast) and deep back-office tools (MarginEdge's invoice OCR plus accounting) are not realistic for a small team.

### Cited Findings
- Vapi advertises $0.05/min and Retell $0.07/min in platform fees. All-in (STT, LLM, TTS, telephony), most setups land at $0.13-0.31/min — [Medium, Automation Labs](https://medium.com/@automation.labs/vapi-vs-retell-vs-bland-in-2026-the-true-cost-per-minute-578f38af3523); [Retell pricing](https://www.retellai.com/pricing); [Cekura](https://www.cekura.ai/blogs/retell-ai-pricing-per-minute)
- Component breakdown: Vapi hosting about $0.05, transcription about $0.01, LLM $0.02-0.20, voice about $0.04, telephony about $0.01 per minute — [Medium](https://medium.com/@automation.labs/vapi-vs-retell-vs-bland-in-2026-the-true-cost-per-minute-578f38af3523); [Ainora](https://ainora.lt/blog/ai-voice-agent-cost-per-minute-2026)
- Vapi, Synthflow and Retell reseller/white-label programs exist but do not publish margin terms — [SeldonFrame](https://www.seldonframe.com/guides/voice-ai-reseller-programs)
- Popmenu's AI answering sends SMS links rather than closing orders on the call. That is a simpler architecture that avoids deep POS integration — [Certus](https://www.certus-ai.com/blogs/popmenu-review)
- Loman claims go-live in under 24 hours at $199/mo, showing that onboarding can be productized — [Loman](https://loman.ai/)

### Inferences
- Unit-economics sketch (illustrative, not sourced): 150-300 AI-handled calls/mo at about 2 minutes is 300-600 minutes, costing about $40-190/mo at $0.13-0.31/min. At a $249-399 price, gross margin is about 50-85%.
- The build path is:
  1. Start with the Popmenu-style design (answer, send an SMS ordering link, take reservations via OpenTable/Resy links).
  2. Add POS order injection later (Toast, Square and Clover APIs).
  3. Sell with a free missed-call audit: forward calls or log call data to show the missed calls before the demo.
- Differentiation against about ten existing vendors, with Owner now bundling AI phone, will be hard nationally. A local, language-specific or agency-bundled offer (voice plus website plus Google Business Profile plus SMS) is more defensible for a small team.

### Gaps
- I found no public data on the true cost of POS integration or certification (Toast partner program) for startups, and no churn data for AI voice products.
