# Pain points of independent restaurant owners — US vs France (2025-2026)

Method note: research date 2026-09-29. Direct page fetches were blocked by the network egress proxy (restaurant.org, altares.com, restaurant365.com), so figures below come from search-result extracts of the cited pages. Numbers should be spot-checked against the primary PDFs before publication. Several sources are vendor blogs (SaaS/POS/accounting firms) and are flagged as such.

## Proposed ranking (top 3 pain points)

1. **Cost inflation squeezing already-thin margins (food + labor + other costs)**, in both countries. US: food costs up 34% vs pre-pandemic. More than 9 in 10 operators call food, labor, insurance, energy and swipe fees significant challenges. 42% of operators were not profitable in 2025 and 33% in H1 2026. Typical net margin is about 3-5%. France: food costs up about 22% since 2021 without full pass-through. INSEE restaurant prices rose +3.9% / +5.3% / +3.1% / +2.4% from 2022 to 2025. Independents' net margin is about 4-10% (vendor estimates).
2. **Staffing: recruiting, retention and turnover.** US: recruiting and retaining staff is the #1 single challenge for 33% of operators (mid-2026, up from 18% at the start of 2026). JOLTS turnover in accommodation and food services was 65.5% in 2025, against a 75.6% long-run average. Replacing an employee costs $1,056 (FOH) to $2,611 (manager). France: 200-300k vacant posts in hotels and restaurants. About 319k hiring projects in 2026. "Recruiting remains the main challenge."
3. **Falling or flat customer traffic, weak consumer purchasing power, and the resulting failures.** France is where this is most acute: 25 restaurant closures per day (UMIH). Traditional-restaurant revenue fell 6.5% year on year in Q2 2026. Restaurant failures rose 8% in 2025 to about 2x early-2022 levels. Visits were down 2% in Oct-Nov 2025. US: foodservice traffic was -0.3% in 2025, with same-store traffic -2.0% in October 2025. Chain Chapter 11 filings accelerated.

Honorable mention (a margin amplifier rather than a top-3 item): delivery-app commissions of 15-30% headline and 30-50% effective. In the Toast survey, marketing/customer acquisition was a top pain point for 16%.

Ranking rationale: every survey places cost and margin first. Staffing is the most-cited *single* #1 problem in the US NRA tracking. In France, traffic and failures have become the dominant 2025-2026 story.

## Q1. Margins, prime cost, food and labor cost % (US vs France)

### Takeaway
In the US, net margins are about 3-5%. Prime cost (food plus labor) is about 60% of sales, split roughly 30/30, and food and labor are each about 33 cents per sales dollar per NRA. A large share of operators are unprofitable: 42% in 2025 and 33% in H1 2026. France's independent net margins are cited at about 4-10%, but only by vendor sources. No official margin benchmark was found.

### Cited Findings
- US: food and labor costs each account for about 33 cents of every dollar in sales. 33% of operators said their restaurant was not profitable in H1 2026 (R365 mid-year report, summarizing NRA data) — [Restaurant365](https://www.restaurant365.com/guides/2026-state-of-the-restaurant-industry-mid-year-report/)
- US: 42% of operators said their business was not profitable in 2025 (NRA 2026 State of the Industry, per secondary summary) — [Smith Allen Group](https://smithallengroup.com/insights/nra-restaurant-industry-record-sales-profitability-crisis-2026/); [NRA report page](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/research-reports/state-of-the-industry/)
- US: prime cost is about 60% of revenue, split roughly evenly, so labor is about 30%. Prime cost of 60% or less is the "gold standard". Example margin of 4%. Fast food runs 6-9% net — [Toast: average restaurant profit margin](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/average-restaurant-profit-margin)
- US: NRA 2026 sales forecast of $1.55T despite cost pressure — [NRA press release](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/media/press-releases/persistent-cost-increases-and-enduring-demand-will-shape-the-restaurant-industry-in-2026/); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/persistent-cost-increases-and-enduring-demand-will-shape-the-restaurant-industry-in-2026-302685661.html)
- France: an independent restaurant's average net margin is 4-9%, with the best reaching 12-15% (vendor blog) — [Ravy](https://ravy.fr/blog/marge-en-restauration) / [Skello](https://www.skello.io/blog/tout-savoir-sur-la-rentabilite-dun-restaurant); another vendor says 5-10% — [bsness.fr](https://bsness.fr/rentabilite-restaurant/); a delivery vendor says 8-12% — [Fooderise](https://www.fooderise.com/commission-plateformes). These estimates conflict and none is primary.
- France: average ticket €23.20 (+2.1%), while food costs rose 22.3% since 2021. The gap not passed on to customers reduced margins — [Extencia chiffres clés 2026](https://www.extencia.fr/chiffres-cles-restauration)
- France: 86-97% of GHR survey respondents (depending on segment) report limited or no investment capacity. More than 1 in 2 businesses say they are fragile or in difficulty — [France24/AFP, 25 Sept 2026](https://www.france24.com/fr/info-en-continu/20260925-faire-son-plein-ou-aller-au-resto-les-restaurants-plomb%C3%A9s-par-les-tensions-sur-le-pouvoir-d-achat)

### Inferences
- With net margins around 3-5% (US), a 1-2 point rise in food or labor cost can wipe out most of the profit. This explains why 33-42% of operators report being unprofitable.
- France's cited margins look higher, but they come from vendor blogs and conflict with GHR/UMIH distress data. Treat them with caution.

### Gaps
- No official French margin benchmark was found (e.g., Banque de France FIBEN or an INSEE ESANE sector ratio). The UMIH plus Ordre des experts-comptables "Observatoire économique" was launched, but its margin figures were not retrieved — [UMIH](https://www.umih.fr/medias/news/un-outil-totalement-inedit-pour-lever-le-voile-sur-la-realite-economique-de-la-restauration.html)
- No French food-cost or labor-cost % benchmark came from a primary source.

## Q2. Food cost inflation 2022-2026 and labor cost growth

### Takeaway
US food costs to restaurants are up 34% vs pre-pandemic. Tariffs and beef (wholesale +10.6% forecast for 2026) keep pressure on. USDA forecasts +4.6% for menu prices (food away from home) in 2026. In France, restaurant consumer prices rose about 15% cumulatively over 2022-2025. INSEE attributes about 45% of the 2021-2023 price increase to labor costs.

### Cited Findings
- US: food costs are up 34% vs pre-pandemic levels. Tariffs drove up prices for proteins, dairy, eggs and produce — [Restaurant365 mid-year 2026](https://www.restaurant365.com/guides/2026-state-of-the-restaurant-industry-mid-year-report/)
- US: 95% of full-service and 94% of limited-service operators call food costs a significant challenge. More than 9 in 10 cite food, labor, insurance, energy and swipe fees as significant challenges — [NRA 2026 SOI via search](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-insights/restaurant-industry-faces-modest-growth-amid-cost-pressures-in-2026); [Bar & Restaurant](https://www.barandrestaurant.com/operations/national-restaurant-association-releases-2026-state-restaurant-industry)
- US: USDA ERS forecasts food-away-from-home prices +4.6% in 2026 and wholesale beef +10.6% (prediction interval 1.6-21.5%). Egg prices fall sharply after avian-flu recovery — [USDA ERS Food Price Outlook](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings); [Hoosier Ag Today, July 2026](https://www.hoosieragtoday.com/2026/07/28/usda-july-2026-food-price-outlook/)
- France: INSEE restaurant and accommodation price inflation was +3.9% (2022), +5.3% (2023), +3.1% (2024) and +2.4% (2025). Fast-food prices peaked at +7.7% in 2023. Labor costs explained about 45% of the sector's price increase over 2021-2023. Food-input inflation moderated to +0.7% in 2025 — [HR-Infos (INSEE analysis)](https://hr-infos.fr/la-hausse-des-prix-en-restauration-et-hebergement-se-maintient-a-22-en-rythme-annuel/)
- France: some raw materials spiked, e.g., oil +300-400% and butter +100% (period unspecified, likely 2022) — [Extencia](https://www.extencia.fr/chiffres-cles-restauration)
- US: operators rank inflation as the top pain point (20%). 48% plan to raise menu prices if inflation continues (51% of FSR operators) — [Toast 2025 Voice of the Restaurant Industry](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/data/2025-voice-of-restaurant-industry-survey)

### Inferences
- US food inflation is re-accelerating in 2026 because of tariffs and beef. French input inflation has cooled, but the cumulative 2021-2023 shock was never fully passed through, so the squeeze persists as a margin issue.

### Gaps
- No precise figures for US restaurant wage growth (e.g., BLS average hourly earnings for food services) or for French labor-cost growth (SMIC revaluations, the HCR wage grid) were retrieved.
- Energy costs in France (a key 2022-2023 shock) were not quantified.

## Q3. Turnover, replacement cost and staffing shortages

### Takeaway
US JOLTS total separations in accommodation and food services were 65.5% in 2025, below the 75.6% long-run average but still about 1.5x the economy-wide rate. Replacing one worker costs about $1,000-2,600. Staffing is again the most-cited single challenge (33% mid-2026). France has 200-300k unfilled posts and turnover of 80-120% in fast-food segments.

### Cited Findings
- US: accommodation and food services annual turnover was 65.5% in 2025 and 65.6% in 2024. The average since 2001 is 75.6%, against 43.4% for the whole economy (BLS JOLTS, via Escoffier analysis) — [Escoffier](https://escoffierglobal.com/blog/restaurant-and-hospitality-industry-annual-turnover-rate/); [BLS JOLTS](https://www.bls.gov/jlt/); [FRED quits series](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/JTU7200QUL)
- US: recruiting and retaining staff as the #1 challenge went 32% (mid-2024) → 24% (late 2024) → 32% (start 2025) → 27% (mid-2025) → 18% (start 2026) → 33% (mid-2026). Nearly three-quarters of operators plan to hire but expect difficulty finding experienced managers and chefs — [Restaurant365 mid-year 2026](https://www.restaurant365.com/guides/2026-state-of-the-restaurant-industry-mid-year-report/)
- US: replacement cost is $1,056 for FOH, $1,491 for BOH and $2,611 for a manager (7shifts survey of 511 operators, Feb 2025) — [7shifts](https://www.7shifts.com/restaurant-labor-costs-playbook); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/7shifts-releases-restaurant-labor-cost-and-profitability-report-to-help-restaurants-adapt-to-industry-challenges-including-inflation-302411115.html)
- US: in the Toast 2025 survey, sourcing/hiring was a top pain point for 16% of operators — [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/data/2025-voice-of-restaurant-industry-survey)
- US: Black Box data shows FSR companies in the lowest turnover quartile had about +1.0 pt better same-store traffic, linking retention to sales — [Black Box Intelligence](https://blackboxintelligence.com/blog/restaurant-industry-in-review-trends-from-october-2025/)
- France: 200,000-300,000 vacant hotel and restaurant posts in summer — [L'Echo touristique](https://www.lechotouristique.com/article/hotellerie-restauration-200-000-a-300-000-postes-vacants-cet-ete); the figure is also cited as 210,000 off-season and up to 300,000 in summer (UMIH / Pôle Emploi) — [Au cœur du CHR](https://aucoeurduchr.fr/article/emploi-formation/ruee-sur-le-recrutement-dans-la-restauration/)
- France: turnover is estimated at 80-120% per year in some fast-food segments. About 319,000 hotel and restaurant hiring projects are expected in 2026 (France Travail BMO, per secondary source) — [Jum Advisory](https://www.jum-advisory.fr/blog/comment-recruter-des-employes-de-restaurant-en-2026); [arkcity](https://www.arkcity.net/gastronomie/restauration-recrutement-difficile-secteur-restaurants.html)
- France: "recruiting remains the main challenge" in the 2025 review — [Hebdo39](https://hebdo39.net/hotellerie-restauration-un-bilan-2025-contraste/)
- France: UMIH wants to recruit Tunisian workers to address labor shortages — [Emploiresto](https://www.emploiresto.com/blog/actualites/restauration-umih-recruter-tunisie)

### Inferences
- US turnover has normalized from the 2021-2022 peaks, but staffing concern rebounded in 2026. Staffing is cyclical and never really resolved.
- The ~70-80% turnover figure in the brief matches the long-run average (75.6%). The 2024-2025 values are lower, at about 65.5%.

### Gaps
- No NRA-specific turnover figure was retrieved. The primary France Travail BMO 2026 "difficultés de recrutement" percentage for cooks and servers was not retrieved (the headline "près d'un recrutement sur deux difficile" comes from a secondary source, arkcity).
- The GHR/UMIH-quoted 200-300k vacancy figure is a union estimate, not an official statistic.

## Q4. Restaurant failures and closures

### Takeaway
France has hard, rising numbers. Altares counted about 70,000 business failures in 2025 (record level). Restaurant failures rose 8% and are about twice early-2022 levels, with 2,119 in Q4 2025 alone. UMIH reports 25 closures per day in 2026. The US has no comparable official count. The evidence is a wave of chain Chapter 11 filings (at least 8 brands in 2025).

### Cited Findings
- France: 69,957 business failures in 2025 (+3.1%), close to the 70,000 peak — [Altares bilan 2025](https://www.altares.com/fr/statistiques-defaillance-entreprises-france/bilan-2025/); [Altares press release](https://www.altares.com/2026/01/20/etude-defaillances-et-sauvegardes-des-entreprises-4eme-trimestre-et-bilan-2025-communique-de-presse/)
- France: 2,119 restaurant failures in Q4 2025 (+8%). Traditional restaurants +7%, fast food +8%. In Q1 2025, table service was +12% and fast food +19%. Restaurants count about twice as many failures as at the start of 2022 — [Altares Q4 2025 PDF](https://altares87872.activehosted.com/content/pyND2/2026/01/19/0c384e52-6925-4cbf-8ae2-eb6f8886a9c3.pdf); [Zepros](https://resto.zepros.fr/actus-metiers/defaillances-2025-restauration-toujours-rouge); [Altares T1 2025](https://www.altares.com/fr/statistiques-defaillance-entreprises-france/bilan-2025/t1/)
- France: 2024 saw a slowdown in restaurant failures (older context) — [Au cœur du CHR](https://aucoeurduchr.fr/article/bars-restaurants/altares-ralentissement-defaillances-restauration/)
- France: UMIH says 25 restaurants close per day. More than half of restaurateurs saw results decline in 2025. Revenue fell in 95 of 98 départements in Q2 2026 — [Epoch Times FR](https://www.epochtimes.fr/25-restaurants-ferment-chaque-jour-les-raisons-derriere-lacceleration-des-fermetures-en-france-3350357.html); [France24/AFP](https://www.france24.com/fr/info-en-continu/20260925-faire-son-plein-ou-aller-au-resto-les-restaurants-plomb%C3%A9s-par-les-tensions-sur-le-pouvoir-d-achat)
- US: at least 8 restaurant brands filed Chapter 11 in 2025, including Hooters, Bertucci's, Bar Louie, On The Border (77 closures), Planta, Bravo Brio and FAT Brands (Jan 2026). Iron Hill filed Chapter 7 — [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/restaurants-closing-us-2025-11034226); [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/restaurants/bankrupt-fat-brands-closes-dozens-of-locations)

### Inferences
- The French restaurant failure count for full-year 2025 is likely about 8,000+ (Q4 was 2,119 and quarters were similar), but this is an extrapolation. The exact annual figure was not retrieved.

### Gaps
- No exact Altares full-year 2025 restaurant failure count was retrieved. No Banque de France sector (NAF 56) figure was retrieved.
- There is no reliable US count of independent restaurant closures for 2025 (Yelp/Datassential/NRA estimates were not found).

## Q5. Delivery commissions and competition

### Takeaway
Headline delivery commissions are 15-30% in the US (DoorDash Basic/Plus/Premier = 15/25/30%) and 25-35% in France (30% standard). Effective cost reaches 30-50% of delivery revenue once promotions and fees are included. That exceeds typical net margins, so delivery orders are often unprofitable for independents.

### Cited Findings
- US: DoorDash Basic/Plus/Premier plans charge 15%/25%/30%, and pickup is 6%. Uber Eats charges 15-30%. Real costs often reach 30-40% per order. Above about 25% effective, most independents stop making money on delivery — [Rezku](https://rezku.com/blog/third-party-delivery-fees-in-2026-what-doordash-uber-eats-grubhub-really-cost-restaurants/); [OPA](https://opalink.com/learn/restaurant-delivery-commission-fees); [KitchenHub](https://www.trykitchenhub.com/post/what-marketplaces-arent-telling-you-how-commission-models-are-quietly-changing)
- US: the Independent Restaurant Coalition is pushing for federal regulation of delivery apps (advocacy source) — [IRC](https://www.independentrestaurantcoalition.com/delivery_apps_2025)
- France: Uber Eats and Deliveroo charge 25-35% of pre-tax turnover, with 30% standard. The total cost including payment and marketing fees is 30-50% of delivery revenue (vendor source) — [Fooderise](https://www.fooderise.com/commission-plateformes); [Commande Ici](https://commandeici.com/blogs/food-business/commission-plateforme-livraison-tableau-comparatif)
- France: DoorDash acquired Deliveroo in 2025. Platforms remain central to strategy in 2026 — [L'Hôtellerie Restauration](https://www.lhotellerie-restauration.fr/actualite/livraison-en-2026-les-plateformes-toujours-au-coeur-de-la-strategie)
- France: table-service restaurants are losing share to more affordable alternatives (fast food, snacking) — [Réussir Les Marchés](https://www.reussir.fr/lesmarches/la-restauration-table-fragilisee-par-des-alternatives-plus-abordables)

### Inferences
- A 30% commission applied to a 3-10% net margin business means delivery orders destroy margin unless prices are marked up. Delivery commissions make the cost squeeze worse rather than being a standalone #1 pain.

### Gaps
- Most commission data comes from vendor blogs, whose business is selling commission-free ordering, so they may be biased. No figure for the share of independent restaurants' revenue that comes from delivery was found. No dark-kitchen data was retrieved.

## Q6. Customer traffic and what owners say is their #1 challenge

### Takeaway
Revenue growth in both countries is price-driven while visits stagnate or fall. France is weaker: visits were down 2% in autumn 2025, 40% of people are limiting restaurant outings for budget reasons, and traditional-restaurant revenue fell 6.5% in Q2 2026. US traffic was -0.3% in 2025. Owner surveys rank inflation/costs first and staffing second, with marketing/customer acquisition close behind (16% in Toast).

### Cited Findings
- US: foodservice traffic was -0.3% in 2025 while spend per visit rose (about +3% in Q4 2025). FSR traffic was +0.6% (Circana) — [Mass Market Retailers / Circana](https://massmarketretailers.com/circana-u-s-foodservice-traffic-dips-in-2025-as-spending-per-visit-rises/)
- US: same-store traffic was -2.0% in October 2025 (Black Box) — [Black Box Intelligence](https://blackboxintelligence.com/blog/restaurant-industry-in-review-trends-from-october-2025/)
- US: the Toast 2025 survey (712 operators, Apr-May 2025) ranked the top pain points as inflation 20%, marketing 16% and sourcing/hiring 16% — [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/data/2025-voice-of-restaurant-industry-survey); [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/toast-survey-inflation-labor-traffic/802424/)
- France: visits were -0.3% and average ticket -0.2% in Q1 2025 (Food Service Vision). Visits were -2% in Oct-Nov 2025, and 1 consumer in 4 says they have cut restaurant spending — [Au cœur du CHR](https://aucoeurduchr.fr/article/national/food-service-vision-restauration-peine/); [Tendances Restauration](https://www.tendances-restauration.com/leger-declin-activite-restauration-automne/)
- France: 11.97bn meals served, stable year on year, while the market grew +4.2% in value. 40% of French people limit restaurant outings for budget reasons — [Extencia](https://www.extencia.fr/chiffres-cles-restauration)
- France: chain restaurants form a €25.5bn market but visits are falling — [L'echommerces](https://lechommerces.fr/restauration-chainee-un-marche-a-255-milliards-deuros-mais-une-frequentation-en-recul/)
- France: average revenue of traditional restaurants fell 6.5% year on year in Q2 2026. Purchasing-power pressure worsened from March 2026 because of the Iran-US conflict and fuel prices — [France24/AFP](https://www.france24.com/fr/info-en-continu/20260925-faire-son-plein-ou-aller-au-resto-les-restaurants-plomb%C3%A9s-par-les-tensions-sur-le-pouvoir-d-achat)

### Inferences
- In France in 2026, falling demand and footfall is arguably the #1 acute pain, ahead of costs. In the US, cost is #1 and staffing is the most-cited single challenge. A combined ranking puts costs/margin #1, staffing #2 and traffic/demand #3 (with failures as the consequence).
- Customer acquisition and retention (marketing at 16% in Toast) is a real but secondary stated pain. It is the most software-addressable of the three.

### Gaps
- No primary UMIH/GIRA survey question ranking "#1 challenge" for French owners was found. The French ranking is inferred from union statements and panel data.
- Square reports and the NRA's own percentage breakdown of challenges were not retrieved (restaurant.org was blocked).
