Method note: research was run on 2026-09-29. ## Q1. Top 10-15 players by revenue / ARR / valuation / locations (with numbers and dates)

### Takeaway
Toast is far ahead of everyone else: $2.4B ARR and about 180,000 locations at Q2 2026, growing about 25%. Square (Block) is the only other player at comparable scale, since food and beverage is its largest vertical at about 35% of GPV. Olo (about 89k locations, taken private for $2.0B in 2025) leads enterprise digital ordering. Among private SMB players, Owner.com grew fastest (from $81M ARR at the end of 2025 to $100M in July 2026). Restaurant365 (about $132M revenue in 2024 [OLD], $1B+ valuation) and MarginEdge (13k+ restaurants, $80M Series D in Aug 2026) lead back-office.

## Q2. Pricing (monthly per location) and business model per player

### Takeaway
The winners monetize payments, not just software. POS players (Toast, Square, SpotOn) advertise $0–$149/month software with card processing around 2.5–2.9% + 15¢, so most revenue is take rate on GPV. Pure SaaS players charge a flat per-location subscription: Owner.com $249–$499, 7shifts $0–$150, Homebase $0–$120. Olo charges enterprise per-location fees (ARPU about $955 per year, roughly $80/month). Its revenue also depends partly on transactions.

### Cited Findings
- Toast: Starter Kit $0/month, Point of Sale $69/month, New Restaurant Basics from $110/month (June 2026). In-person processing is 2.49% + 15¢. Pay-as-you-go runs 2.99% + 15¢ on a 2-year agreement — [POSUSA](https://www.posusa.com/toast-pos-pricing/); [Chowly](https://chowly.com/resources/blogs/toast-vs-square-for-restaurants/)
- Toast model: recurring gross profit = subscription + fintech. Q2 2026 recurring GP was $595M — [Toast 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001650164/000165016426000162/tost-20260630xexhibit991.htm)
- Square for Restaurants: Free $0, Plus $49/month per location, Premium $149/month per location (June 2026). Processing on Free is 2.6% + 15¢ in person and 3.3% + 30¢ online. Plus lowers this to 2.5% + 15¢ and 2.9% + 30¢ — [POSUSA Toast vs Square](https://www.posusa.com/toast-vs-square/)
- SpotOn: Quick Start $0 (2.79% + 15¢), paid plans from $55/month (1.99% + 25¢). A typical single location pays $0–$135/month in software — [Sonary](https://sonary.com/reviews/spoton/); [POSUSA](https://www.posusa.com/toast-vs-spoton/)
- Owner.com: flat $499/month with no commission, or a Flex plan at $249/month plus 5% per order. Restaurants can pass a 5% convenience fee to diners — [Sauce](https://www.getsauce.com/post/owner-com-pricing-fees); [Restolabs](https://www.restolabs.com/blog/owner-com-pricing); [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/owner)
- Olo: ARPU about $955 in Q2 2025, +12% YoY. This is annualized per location (per Olo's definition, about $80/month) — [Olo Q2 2025 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1431695/000143169525000022/oloq22025exhibit991.htm)
- 7shifts: Comp $0 (1 location, ≤30 staff), Entree $34.99, The Works $76.99, Gourmet $150 per location per month — [Shifton comparison](https://shifton.com/comparison/homebase-vs-7shifts/); [ustechautomations](https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-7shifts-vs-homebase-2026)
- Homebase: free plan (≤20 employees), paid from $24/location/month. Plus is $70 and All-in-One $120 per location per month — [Shifton](https://shifton.com/comparison/homebase-vs-7shifts/)
- Restaurant365: average ACV about $3.3K per customer (2024 est.) [OLD] — [Getlatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/restaurant365)

### Inferences
- There are 3 business-model archetypes:
  - (a) POS + payments (Toast, Square, SpotOn): the software is a loss leader and the take rate is the money.
  - (b) Flat SaaS per location (Owner, 7shifts, Homebase, MarginEdge, R365, MarketMan, meez): typically $25–$500/month.
  - (c) Enterprise usage/transaction-based (Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, SoundHound): priced per location or per order.
- Owner's $499 flat fee is framed as an alternative to DoorDash's roughly 15–30% commissions. That commission range is a common industry figure, not sourced here.

### Gaps
- Official list prices not found: MarginEdge (commonly cited around $330/location/month, unverified), Restaurant365, MarketMan, meez, Craftable, Popmenu, BentoBox, Thanx, Punchh, Paytronix, SoundHound, Presto, Slang.ai, Loman and Tattle. Many are quote-only.

## Q4. What makes the winners succeed

### Takeaway
The winners combine five things:
- a restaurant-only vertical focus
- an all-in-one bundle anchored on payments (Toast)
- a hard-dollar ROI pitch: stop paying commissions (Owner), cut food cost (MarginEdge/R365), capture missed calls (voice AI)
- heavy direct or local field sales for SMBs
- quick, simple setup

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The success formula has four parts:
  - (1) Own the transaction: payments or orders give usage-based revenue that grows with the restaurant.
  - (2) Promise a single hard-dollar ROI: commission savings, food-cost %, labor %, recovered calls.
  - (3) Keep onboarding low-friction (a free or $0 entry tier, done-for-you setup).
  - (4) Bundle or integrate deeply with the POS, since POS owners such as Toast (xtraCHEF, AI) keep absorbing adjacent categories.
- For a new entrant (for example in France), the most defensible wedges are ones where Toast/Square are weak: independent-restaurant marketing and direct ordering (the Owner playbook) and AI voice/phone. Commoditized scheduling is the least defensible.
