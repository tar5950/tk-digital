## Q1. Vertical vs horizontal: why do verticals win with SMBs (and the "vertical SaaS + fintech = 2-5x ARPU" thesis)?

### Takeaway
The dominant VC thesis (a16z, Tidemark, Bessemer) is that vertical SaaS wins with SMBs by owning a "control point" (the system run last before closing: POS for restaurants), then monetizing through embedded fintech that multiplies revenue per customer 2-5x. Restaurants are the textbook case: Toast earns more from payments than from software, and every successful local-business vertical (ServiceTitan, Mindbody, Jobber) follows the same payments → lending → payroll sequence.

### Cited Findings
- a16z "Fintech Scales Vertical SaaS": adding fintech can increase revenue per customer by 2-5x (the source of the widely quoted multiplier; a16z page itself was not fetchable, figure confirmed via secondary citations) — [a16z](https://a16z.com/fintech-scales-vertical-saas/); [Apideck](https://www.apideck.com/blog/embedded-finance-vertical-saas)
- Illustrative math used by practitioners: a vertical tool charging $200/month in software can capture ~$800/month more from payments, payroll, capital advances and insurance (~$1,000 total) — [Fractal Software, Vertical SaaS Fintech Playbook](https://www.fractalsoftware.com/perspectives/the-vertical-saas-fintech-playbook) (via search snippet; illustrative, not measured)
- The playbook order is nearly identical across winners: embedded payments first, then lending, then insurance/banking/payroll/cards ("the playbook Toast, Shopify and Bill made famous") — [Fractal Software](https://www.fractalsoftware.com/perspectives/the-vertical-saas-fintech-playbook)
- Jobber can profitably serve tiny contractors at ~1/3 of ServiceTitan's price because modest SaaS fee + % of payments/financing creates viable LTV — [Fractal Software](https://www.fractalsoftware.com/perspectives/the-vertical-saas-fintech-playbook)
- ServiceTitan revenue mix: subscription ~71%, usage-based fintech ~25%, professional services ~4%; added Affirm BNPL in Sept 2025; $1B+ ARR, ~110% NRR, fintech the fastest-growing line — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/5-interesting-learnings-from-servicetitan-at-1b-in-arr/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/servicetitan/)
- Mindbody (salons/fitness): payments alone >1/3 of revenue in the 3 years before its 2015 IPO; later cited as >half of revenue from embedded financial products — [Fractal Software / Mindbody S-1 via search](https://www.fractalsoftware.com/perspectives/the-vertical-saas-fintech-playbook); [Apideck](https://www.apideck.com/blog/embedded-finance-vertical-saas)
- Shopify: merchant solutions (payments, lending, banking) ~73% of revenue — [Apideck](https://www.apideck.com/blog/embedded-finance-vertical-saas)
- Toast: ARR split roughly half subscription (~$1.06B) / half payments (~$986M), NRR 109% (FY2025) — [BusinessWire FY2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260212058106/en/Toast-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results) (from the existing report). Apideck claims Toast "made $5B from financial services" vs $936M subscriptions — this is gross payments revenue (before processing costs) and should not be compared with ARR — [Apideck](https://www.apideck.com/blog/embedded-finance-vertical-saas)
- Toast Capital: loans $1k-$300k underwritten on POS/payment data, ~$1B cumulative originations cited; Toast frames payments + lending as a retention driver — [Toast Capital](https://pos.toasttab.com/products/capital); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2024/toast-says-payments-lending-are-digital-recipe-for-retention/); [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/financing/funding-hard-come-some-restaurants-turn-their-tech-suppliers)
- Tidemark VSKP framework: vertical SaaS is "inherently multi-product", can reach high market share and network effects; the key concept is the "control point" = software a customer turns off last / holds the most critical data — [Tidemark VSKP](https://www.tidemarkcap.com/vskp); [Tidemark intro](https://www.tidemarkcap.com/post/introducing-the-vertical-saas-knowledge-project); benchmarks: [Tidemark 2025 Vertical & SMB SaaS Benchmark](https://www.tidemarkcap.com/vskp-chapter/2025-vertical-smb-saas-benchmark-report)
- Bessemer (vertical AI, 2024-25): industries labelled "technophobic" were not resistant to tech; SaaS failed to address their language-heavy, multimodal tasks (calls, messages, documents). AI now "feels less like software and more like real leverage" — [Bessemer Part I: The future of AI is vertical](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/part-i-the-future-of-ai-is-vertical); [Bessemer ten principles](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/part-iv-ten-principles-for-building-strong-vertical-ai-businesses)
- Other quoted uplifts (vendor/secondary, lower confidence): embedded accounting +38% ARPU, +20% LTV (Open Ledger, a vendor) — [Open Ledger](https://www.openledger.com/openledger-hq/ultimate-guide-to-embedded-fintech-solutions-for-vertical-saas-open-ledger)

### Inferences
- For restaurants the control point is the POS + payments. This explains why Toast/Square absorb adjacent categories (payroll, capital, invoice/AP) and why standalone point tools are structurally exposed to bundling.
- Local-business analogs (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Mindbody) show the transferable pattern: (1) own a daily workflow, (2) attach payments, (3) add capital/BNPL, (4) move downmarket with lower SaaS price subsidized by take rate. Jobber at 1/3 of ServiceTitan's price is the "independent-SMB" version, the closest analog for independent restaurants (kebabs, pizzerias).
- Bessemer's framing matters for restaurants: phone calls, reviews, supplier invoices and messages are exactly the "language-heavy" tasks that pre-AI SaaS failed to capture. This is why AI voice (Slang, Loman) and invoice OCR (MarginEdge) are the categories being transposed now.

- No restaurant-specific ARPU uplift figure (software only vs software + payments) for independents outside Toast's aggregate.
- No sourced 2025-26 figure for Toast Capital originations; the ~$1B figure is older.

## Q2. Category-by-category mapping: generic winner → restaurant equivalent, and evidence of success/failure

### Takeaway
Nearly every horizontal category has a restaurant equivalent in the US. The ones that succeed attach to a revenue line the owner already watches (direct orders, covers, calls, food cost), or they are absorbed by POS/marketplace platforms (SevenRooms → DoorDash, xtraCHEF → Toast). Standalone horizontal tools (Mailchimp, Wix, Birdeye, Gusto) persist among restaurants but lose to verticals that bundle channels plus data from the POS.

### Inferences
- **Transposes well:** e-commerce/website (Shopify/Wix → Owner), CRM/marketing (HubSpot → SevenRooms/Thanx/Owner), HR scheduling (Gusto → 7shifts/Skello), AP/invoice (Bill → MarginEdge), AI receptionist (Smith.ai → Slang/Loman), embedded lending (Square Loans → Toast Capital). Common factor: a measurable dollar outcome plus a data feed from POS/invoices/calls.
- **Transposes poorly or gets absorbed:** dynamic pricing (consumer backlash, political risk), pure procurement marketplaces (restaurant side does not pay; value shifts to distributors), standalone pay-at-table (pivoted/shrank before finding a premium-dining niche).
- **Exit pattern:** strong verticalized point solutions tend to end up inside a platform (SevenRooms → DoorDash; the same pattern appears with xtraCHEF → Toast, see Gaps). A new standalone French tool should expect POS or marketplace players to become acquirers or bundlers.
- Owner and Skello both started in restaurants and are now broadening ("every local business", "frontline HR"). Restaurants work as a beachhead for local-business SaaS, not necessarily as the end market.

## Q3. What doesn't transpose: limits, failures, churn, and white spaces (US and France)

### Takeaway
Restaurants inherit SMB churn plus a very high involuntary churn from closures (~17% fail in year one, roughly half of the TAM closes within five years). That punishes pure-software models with high CAC and rewards payments-attached or ROI-provable tools. In France the clearest white spaces are AI voice ordering for independents (only small, unfunded players), AP/invoice automation for independents timed to the Sept 2026 e-invoicing obligation, and embedded lending, which has no Toast Capital equivalent.

### Cited Findings
- Restaurant-specific churn: ~17% of restaurants fail in year 1, and nearly half of the TAM closes within 5 years. This drives involuntary churn for vendors — [Datalane, Canonical Guide to Selling to Restaurants](https://www.datalane.com/guides/the-canonical-guide-to-selling-to-restaurants)
- Generic SMB SaaS benchmarks: healthy monthly logo churn 2-4% for SMB, and 3-5% monthly tolerated for ACV <$10k (secondary benchmark blogs, low confidence) — [Vantainsights](https://vantainsights.com/insights/saas-churn-rate); [Optifai](https://optif.ai/learn/questions/b2b-saas-churn-rate-benchmark/); [Recurly](https://recurly.com/research/churn-rate-benchmarks/)
- Toast attributes retention partly to payments + lending (fintech as churn reducer) — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2024/toast-says-payments-lending-are-digital-recipe-for-retention/)
- Sunday shows the enterprise/VC-scale mismatch: $124M raised in 2021, then retreat from 60% of markets within ~1 year — [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/restaurant-payments-app-sunday-cuts-staff-exits-60-of-its-markets/627312/)
- Price/consumer acceptance limit: dynamic pricing backlash (Wendy's) — [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/wendys-backtracks-on-dynamic-pricing-after-consumer-backlash/708799/)
- France e-invoicing: from 1 Sept 2026 all VAT-registered restaurants must be able to *receive* e-invoices via an approved platform (Plateforme Agréée). Issuance is required for SMEs/TPE from 1 Sept 2027 (ETI/large groups from Sept 2026) — [UMIH FAQ](https://www.umih.fr/medias/news/foire-aux-questions-la-facturation-electronique.html); [Zelty](https://blog.zelty.fr/facturation-electronique-restaurant-2026); [Pennylane](https://www.pennylane.com/fr/fiches-pratiques/facture-electronique/facturation-electronique-pour-les-restaurants-ce-quil-faut-savoir)
- France digital adoption (search snippets from France Num 2025 barometer + a Lightspeed study; page not fetchable): ~76% of hospitality TPE/PME have POS software, ~58% accounting software, ~53% invoicing software, ~20% use AI (+12 pts YoY); Lightspeed: 72% of restaurateurs adopted digital tools (contactless, QR, all-in-one POS) — [France Num 2025](https://www.francenum.gouv.fr/guides-et-conseils/strategie-numerique/comprendre-le-numerique/barometre-france-num-2025-le-0); [TendanceHotellerie / Lightspeed](https://www.tendancehotellerie.fr/articles-breves/communique-de-presse/18917-article/nouvelle-etude-lightspeed-72-des-restaurateurs-ont-adopte-des-outils-digitaux-pour-faire-face-a-l-inflation-et-a-la-penurie-de-main-d-oeuvre) (unverified attribution of each % to each study)
- France AI voice for restaurants: a fragmented set of small players (Vocalis, Yumcall, Accueil IA, AirAgent, Tala, Repply). No funding round was found in search. Case-study claims (e.g. +28 bookings/week for a Paris starred restaurant) are vendor marketing — [Vocalis](https://vocalis.pro/agent-vocal-ia-restaurant); [Yumcall](https://yumcall.app/fonctionnalites/agent-vocal-ia); [Accueil IA](https://www.accueil-ia.fr/agent-vocal-ia/); [AirAgent](https://airagent.fr/restauration/)

### Inferences
- **What doesn't transpose:** (1) enterprise complexity and implementation-heavy ERP-like products for independents (R365-type suites fit chains). (2) Consumer-facing pricing tech from airlines/hotels, because diners punish visible price changes. (3) Marketplace models where the restaurant is the payer but gets little value (Choco moved to the distributor side). (4) VC-scale blitz rollouts before unit economics (Sunday 2022).
- **Churn design implication:** with ~half of restaurants closing within 5 years, CAC payback must be short (Toast ~15 months per existing report). Low-touch sales plus a monthly ROI proof (calls recovered, commissions saved) are needed. Payments or lending attachments raise switching costs.
- **White spaces, US:** fewer, since most categories are covered. Remaining gaps are an integrated "AI back-office" for independent QSR (vs chains) and non-surge menu/price optimization framed as discounts.
- **White spaces, France:** (a) AI voice ordering for fast-food independents (unfunded, fragmented); (b) invoice capture + food cost for independents riding the Sept 2026 e-invoice reception mandate. MarginEdge-style, bundled with a Plateforme Agréée, the mandate turns a "nice-to-have" into a compliance trigger; (c) embedded revenue-based lending on POS data (no French Toast Capital identified); (d) an Owner-like direct-ordering + marketing bundle for kebab/pizzeria independents (see existing report). These are inferences from absence of funded players in searches, not proof of absence.

### Gaps
- No restaurant-SaaS-specific churn figures (e.g., 7shifts, Owner logo churn) publicly found.
- Could not verify France Num percentages directly (site blocked). Attribution between France Num and Lightspeed figures is uncertain.
- No search done on French embedded lending (e.g., revenue-based financing players) or French AP tools (Libeo, Pennylane) specifically targeting restaurants. The absence of a "French Toast Capital" is unconfirmed.

## Q4. How the French market imitates US models, and the typical US → France lag

### Takeaway
French restaurant tech mirrors US categories with a lag that varies by category, from none to several years. Pay-at-table (Sunday) and reputation management (Malou) launched roughly in parallel with or after US equivalents and even export to New York. Scheduling/HR (Skello ≈ 7shifts/Homebase) and reservations+CRM (Zenchef ≈ SevenRooms/OpenTable) have scaled via European roll-ups rather than fintech. France lacks a dominant Toast-style POS+payments control point, which limits the "vertical SaaS + fintech" multiplier and keeps players point-solution or multi-country consolidators.

### Cited Findings
- Sunday: founded 2021 by Big Mamma's co-founder (Victor Lugger), launched simultaneously in FR/US/UK. By 2025 the US is ~1,000 of >3,000 restaurants, and the new $21M raise is aimed at US acceleration — [Boursorama/AFP](https://www.boursorama.com/actualite-economique/actualites/restauration-l-application-de-paiement-par-qr-code-sunday-veut-doubler-de-taille-en-2026-9c32a9621e69776fb2f936142df337ed); [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/restaurant-payments-app-sunday-cuts-staff-exits-60-of-its-markets/627312/)
- Malou: founded 2021 (restaurant-vertical reputation/visibility, the Birdeye/Podium analog), €10M Nov 2023, expanding to New York — [Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2023/11/20/foodtech-malou-leve-10-millions-deuros-pour-simposer-a-paris-et-a-new-york/); [JDN (earlier €7M round)](https://www.journaldunet.com/retail/1526345-le-francais-malou-leve-7-millions-d-euros-pour-ameliorer-la-visibilite-des-restaurants-newyorkais/)
- Skello (7shifts/Homebase analog): >€50M ARR, profitable, €200M Bridgepoint (2026). The growth plan relies on European acquisitions (4-5 targets identified) — [Bridgepoint](https://www.bridgepointgroup.com/about-us/news-and-insights/press-releases/2026/skello-secures-a-new-eur200-million-investment-led-by-Bridgepoint-to-support-its-European-growth); [FrenchWeb](https://www.frenchweb.fr/skello-leve-200-millions-deuros-la-french-tech-entre-dans-lere-des-consolidateurs-europeens/462695)
- Zenchef (SevenRooms/OpenTable analog): consolidation strategy (Formitable merger, Tablebooker Feb 2024, CoverManager July 2025), targeting 35,000 European restaurants — [MandA.be](https://manda.be/articles/zenchef-continues-consolidation-with-acquisition-of-tablebooker/); [Neorestauration](https://www.neorestauration.com/article/zenchef-s-associe-a-l-entreprise-neerlandaise-formitable-pour-creer-un-leader-europeen-de-la-restaurant-tech,67509); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/acquisitions/acquisitions-by-zenchef/__4rDJ9iBBN8TjD5H8MWIB7YPh-tEeEWjxdLjM1M3TRCI)
- Innovorder (Olo-like ordering/kiosk for chains): €20M raise, profitable for ~2.5 years, ~40% growth, revenue €11.5M (2025) → €15M projected (2026) — [Snacking.fr](https://www.snacking.fr/actualites/8119-Apres-sa-levee-de-20-M-Innovorder-vise-le-leadership-europeen/)
- US category leaders are being bought by platforms (SevenRooms → DoorDash, $1.2B). DoorDash also bought Deliveroo the same day, making it a direct actor in France — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/06/doordash-announces-1point2-billion-sevenrooms-deal-misses-revenue-expectations.html)

### Inferences
- **Typical lag:** there is no single number. Category-by-category, the gap between a US vertical's rise and a French funded equivalent looks like ~0-3 years for hospitality-specific ideas (pay-at-table, reputation, AI voice now) and longer for fintech-heavy models (a Toast-style POS+payments+capital stack has not emerged in France). This is an inference from founding/raise dates, not a measured statistic.
- French winners are profitable, capital-efficient consolidators (Skello, Innovorder, Zenchef) targeting chains and multi-site groups, not VC blitz models. That fits thinner French margins and a fragmented market.
- DoorDash's ownership of SevenRooms + Deliveroo makes it plausible that US-style marketplace-owned CRM gets pushed into France. This is a risk for independent French CRM/reservation tools, and it also supports selling "direct channel ownership" to restaurateurs.

- No quantitative study of the US→France SaaS lag was found.
- Les Echos articles not retrieved. French funding amounts for Zenchef (total raised, ARR) were not verified in this pass.
- Did not verify whether any French POS (Zelty, Innovorder, Lightspeed FR, SumUp) offers embedded lending at scale.
